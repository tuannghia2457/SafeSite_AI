# Startup Thesis

Can SafeSite AI be a startup? **Yes, if it is positioned as a safety-evidence layer, not a helmet detector or an FDI story.** This is based on [research/LITERATURE_REVIEW.md](research/LITERATURE_REVIEW.md), with numbers checked in [research/FACT_CHECK.md](research/FACT_CHECK.md).

## Positioning

> **Vietnam doesn't need another helmet detector. It needs a trusted safety-evidence layer.**

**EN pitch:** Vietnam is attracting global investment rapidly, but Vietnamese contractors and suppliers still need to prove they can operate to the safety expectations of international clients. We turn day-to-day safety operations into verifiable evidence.

**VI pitch:** Việt Nam không thiếu cơ hội từ FDI; thách thức là làm sao để nhiều doanh nghiệp Việt Nam tham gia sâu hơn vào các dự án và chuỗi cung ứng đó. An toàn lao động là một trong các điều kiện để được tin tưởng, vượt qua vòng sơ tuyển và duy trì hợp đồng. Chúng tôi biến hoạt động an toàn hằng ngày thành dữ liệu và bằng chứng mà doanh nghiệp có thể chứng minh với đối tác quốc tế.

## Initial customer (ICP)

Vietnamese contractors and subcontractors working for FDI developers, multinational EPCs, IFC/World Bank-linked projects or multinational manufacturers. This is where the pain of being unsafe and the pain of *proving* safety meet. The next segment is export factories with recurring EHS/social audits.

## What we must prove

1. International contracts require safety evidence. **Supported:** IFC GPN 2017 ¶34–37, IFC PS2.
2. Vietnamese firms struggle to produce that evidence. **Needs customer discovery:** 15–20 interviews.
3. Our platform measurably reduces that friction and improves safety management. **Needs pilot data:** see the metrics in the review.

Metric to steer by: **Verified Safety Closure Rate**, the share of validated high-risk cases closed with evidence within SLA.

## Claims we don't make

| Don't say | Why | Say instead |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| "Vietnam loses FDI / runs a trade deficit because workplaces are unsafe" | VN had a US$24.77B surplus and record US$25.35B realized FDI in 2024; no causal estimate exists | "Vietnamese firms need to prove safety to win and keep international work" |
| "Vietnam loses $X billion of FDI per year to safety" | No study supports any such number | Use firm-level outcomes: prequalification pass rate, audit findings, prep hours |
| "Our AI certifies ISO 45001 compliance" | ISO 45001 is a management-system standard; certification needs an audit; the standard is being revised for 2027 | "We generate evidence that supports ISO 45001 and client audits" |
| "Accidents drop 50% in 3 months" | Serious accidents are rare; a small pilot has no statistical power | Report leading indicators: closure time, repeat violations, audit findings |
| "Nobody has done AI safety monitoring" | Intenseye, Protex AI, viAct and Voxel exist, and helmet detection reaches 97% precision in papers | "Detection is a commodity; our moat is workflow + evidence + contractor trust" |
| "EU law forces Vietnamese suppliers to buy this now" | CSDDD scope was cut by Omnibus I; it applies from July 2029 to very large firms only | "Due-diligence expectations are rising; buyers already audit suppliers" |
| "Thailand blacklisted the contractor" | Termination and blacklisting were ordered; contracts were suspended; final termination went to legal review | "Thailand ordered termination and suspended contracts after fatal crane collapses" |

## Customer-discovery questions

- In your last three international tenders, which HSE documents were required?
- Have you ever lost points or failed prequalification because of missing HSE records?
- Do buyers ask for TRIFR, fatalities, training hours, audit findings, ISO 45001 or corrective-action records?
- How many person-hours does the HSE dossier take?
- How many audit findings came from evidence scattered across Excel, Zalo, paper or cameras?
- Can the buyer suspend you if safety KPIs drop?
- If we cut evidence preparation time by 70%, whose budget pays for it: HSE, IT or procurement?
