# SafeSite AI

**AI-Powered Workplace Safety & Compliance Platform**

*Safer Workplaces. Globally Ready.*

## Project Overview

SafeSite AI is an AI-powered workplace safety management platform designed to help Vietnamese construction companies, factories, and industrial contractors improve workplace safety and strengthen their readiness for international business partnerships.

The platform combines Computer Vision, safety incident management, and bilingual compliance reporting into one integrated system.

## The Problem

Many businesses still rely on manual safety inspections, CCTV monitoring, and fragmented documentation to manage workplace safety.

This can make it difficult to detect potential hazards, track incidents, manage corrective actions, and provide reliable safety records to international partners.

## Our Solution

SafeSite AI provides three core features:

1. **AI Safety Monitoring:** Uses Computer Vision and existing CCTV infrastructure to identify potential safety violations, such as missing helmets, missing safety vests, and entry into restricted areas.

2. **Safety Management System:** Enables safety officers to review alerts, record incidents, assign corrective actions, and track their resolution.

3. **Compliance & Reporting:** Generates structured Vietnamese-English safety reports with supporting evidence to assist internal management, audits, and international business communication.

## Target Customers

- Construction companies and contractors.
- Manufacturing factories and industrial facilities.
- Businesses operating in industrial parks.
- Vietnamese suppliers working with international partners.

## Value Proposition

SafeSite AI aims to help businesses:

- Improve workplace safety monitoring.
- Reduce manual safety management workload.
- Digitize incident records and corrective actions.
- Improve transparency and accountability.
- Prepare more consistent safety documentation for international partners.

## Business Model

SafeSite AI is envisioned as a **B2B SaaS platform**, with subscription plans based on the number of sites, cameras, and selected features.

## Current Development Stage

The project is currently in the **prototype stage**.

The initial demo will focus on a safety dashboard, simulated AI detection, incident management, and bilingual reporting. Real Computer Vision models, such as YOLO, will be integrated and evaluated in later development phases.

## Our Vision

To help Vietnamese enterprises build safer workplaces, demonstrate responsible safety management, and strengthen trust with international business partners.
